# Enterprise CXO Account Profiling & IT Market Intelligence

### Enterprise IT Leadership — Mumbai & MMR

> **Enterprise B2B market research and account profiling project focused on identifying, validating, and segmenting CIO, CTO, CISO, and CDO contacts across large enterprise organizations.**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on **enterprise B2B account profiling and IT market intelligence** across Mumbai and the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR).

The objective was to identify enterprise organizations based on strict **geography, employee-size, department, and seniority criteria**, and map their most relevant IT decision-makers.

Unlike a broad prospecting exercise, this project followed a **CXO-only targeting strategy**, focusing exclusively on:

- **CIO — Chief Information Officer**
- **CTO — Chief Technology Officer**
- **CISO — Chief Information Security Officer**
- **CDO — Chief Digital / AI Officer**

The project demonstrates an end-to-end **B2B data prospecting workflow**, covering account discovery, CXO research, data cleaning, title standardization, validation, enrichment, segmentation, and CRM-ready structuring.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The core business question was:

> **How can we identify enterprise organizations in Mumbai/MMR and accurately map their senior-most IT decision-makers while maintaining strict CXO-level and enterprise-size criteria?**

### Targeting Criteria

- **Geography:** Mumbai & Mumbai Metropolitan Region
- **Company Type:** Enterprise Organizations
- **Employee Size:** 2,500+ Employees
- **Department:** IT
- **Seniority:** CXO Only
- **Target Titles:** CIO, CTO, CISO, CDO

## 🔍 Research Methodology

The project followed a structured **B2B data prospecting and validation workflow**.

### **01 — Brief & Criteria Definition**

Defined the target market using:

- Geography
- Enterprise employee threshold
- IT department
- CXO-level seniority
- Target executive titles

### **02 — Account & Contact Discovery**

Used targeted filters across professional B2B research platforms to identify qualifying enterprise accounts and IT executives.

### **03 — Data Cleaning & Standardization**

Processed the collected records using Microsoft Excel.

Key activities included:

- Duplicate removal
- Job-title standardization
- Removal of non-CXO profiles
- Record structuring
- Data categorization

### **04 — Validation & Enrichment**

Cross-validated contact information and title accuracy using multiple data sources.

A key validation step was distinguishing genuine CXO-level positions from VP-level or "Head of IT" roles that could otherwise be incorrectly classified as C-suite profiles.

### **05 — CRM-Ready Structuring**

Organized the validated records into a standardized structure suitable for:

- CRM upload
- Team circulation
- Account segmentation
- Executive-level outreach

## 💡 Key Insights

- **CIOs represented the largest CXO segment**, accounting for 45% of the final contact database, followed by CTOs at 30%.

- **Infrastructure & Cloud was the largest IT ownership segment**, representing 35% of the mapped contacts, followed by Cybersecurity & Risk at 25%.

- **Data, Analytics & AI/ML represented 20% of the contact base**, providing a distinct segment of enterprise technology leadership associated with data and AI ownership.

- **50% of the target accounts were within the 2,500–5,000 employee range**, while the remaining accounts were distributed across larger enterprise tiers.

- **The average of 1.5 CXO contacts per account reflects the project's highly focused targeting strategy**, rather than maximizing the number of contacts per organization.

- **The research prioritized role accuracy over volume**, with the final dataset restricted to CIO, CTO, CISO, and CDO positions.
